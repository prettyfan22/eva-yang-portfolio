Off the clock

At heart, I'm Wuhanese.Nothing beats a bowl of 热干面 (reganmian): hot dry noodles coated in an equally dry sesame paste, born on the Hankou docks in the 1930s as a quick, filling breakfast for dockworkers.

From there, most of what I love comes down to exploring: new scents, new flavours, new places.

Smell
For perfume, I reach for santal, pink pepper rose and osmanthus: cool, woody, unisex scents that help me unwind. They're easy to share with almost anyone, and quietly say calm, mature and capable. At home, it's bergamot and rose oils.
Sip
My mornings start with an iced pour-over, made with Geisha beans if my budget worked out that month. My evenings end with a glass of French or Napa Chardonnay.
Savour
On weekends I bake rosemary and olive focaccia, mostly because I love the smell of the dough as it ferments. Food is my favourite way to get to know a culture, so I'll happily eat my way through Chinese, Korean, Japanese and Italian kitchens.
Play
I recently traded the gym for badminton, and it turned out to be the perfect outlet for my competitive side. I get at least one practice in every week in LA, all so I can beat some uncs and friends back home in Vancouver and Calgary.
Wander
I love getting lost somewhere new: the concrete jungle of New York, cyberpunk Chongqing, K-drama Seoul, and every themed café I find along the way.
